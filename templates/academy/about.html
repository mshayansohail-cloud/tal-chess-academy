Our Story

Founded by competitors, for competitors.

TAL Chess Academy was founded by a group of titled players who shared one frustration: most chess instruction stops at "how the pieces move" and never builds toward the calculation, preparation, and composure a rated tournament actually demands.

We built the Academy around a simple structure — clear levels, a defined curriculum at each one, and coaches who are still active tournament competitors themselves. Students don't just learn theory; they learn how a title-holder actually thinks at the board.

Today the Academy trains juniors and adults across five programmes, runs its own internal tournament calendar, and has produced dozens of rated and nationally ranked players.
