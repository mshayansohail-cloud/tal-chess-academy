Questions, answered.

The things people usually want to know before they book. If yours isn't here, just ask us — we reply within one business day.

I've never played chess before. Can I still join?

Yes. The Junior Program and the Beginner programme both start from the rules of the board — no prior experience assumed, and no expectation that you arrive knowing anything. Our trial form has "Never played" as an option precisely because that's where a lot of our students start.

Do you teach adults, or only children?

Both. We train juniors and adults alike, from a first lesson through a first rated tournament and beyond. The Junior Program is age-specific (6–11); every other programme is organised by playing strength rather than age, so an adult beginner and a teenage beginner can sit in the same room without either being out of place.

How do I know which programme is right for me?

You don't need to work it out in advance — that's what the trial is for. Book a trial lesson and we'll place you at the right level after a short assessment across the board. If you'd like to make an informed guess first, our programmes are banded by rating: Intermediate is aimed at 1200–1800, Advanced at 1800+, and Beginner at anyone new to competitive play — but those bands are a starting guide, not a strict cutoff, since a 1250-rated player and a 1750-rated player often need quite different things from the same term.

What actually happens at a trial lesson?

Three steps. You tell us about your (or your child's) chess experience and pick a programme on the enquiry form. We reply within one business day to schedule a time. Then you sit across the board with a coach for a short assessment, and leave with a clear sense of which programme fits — whether or not you decide to continue with us.

How long does a programme run?

The Junior, Beginner and Intermediate programmes run in 12-week terms. Advanced runs in 12-week terms on an ongoing basis, since preparation at that level doesn't have a natural finish line. Private Coaching is flexible and booked per session rather than by term.

Are lessons taught in groups or one-to-one?

Our four levelled programmes are taught in small groups — deliberately small, so that game reviews are detailed and individual weaknesses actually get addressed rather than averaged away. If you'd prefer training built entirely around your own games, Private Coaching is one-to-one with a coach of your choosing.

Will I get to play competitively?

Yes. We run internal tournaments regularly so there's real competitive pressure between external events, and students train under classical time controls before they ever sit a rated game. Our events calendar lists what's coming up.

Where are you based, and when are you open?

We're based in Karachi, at Academy Hall, Downtown Centre. Our hours are Monday to Saturday, 9am to 7pm. Full contact details are on our contact page.
