02 / About the Academy

We believe chess is taught best as a discipline — not a hobby squeezed in on weekends.

TAL Chess Academy was founded on the conviction that serious improvement requires structure: a real curriculum, real feedback, and coaches who have competed at the level students aspire to. We train juniors and adults alike, from their very first lesson through their first rated tournament and beyond.

Every programme is built around the same principle — depth over breadth. Fewer openings, understood properly. Fewer positions, calculated thoroughly. It is a slower path, and it is the one that produces players who can actually hold their own across the board.
